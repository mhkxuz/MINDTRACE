Behavioral Features and their corresponding
formulas:
1.​ user_turn_count
Count of rows where speaker == "user" for that conversation_id Example:
Conversation 1 has 25 total turns, 13 are user → user_turn_count = 13

2.​ assistant_turn_count
Count of rows where speaker == "assistant" for that conversation_id
Example: Conversation 1 has 25 total turns, 12 are assistant →
assistant_turn_count = 12

3.​ total_turn_count
Total rows for that conversation_id regardless of speaker Formula:
user_turn_count + assistant_turn_count = total_turn_count Example: 13 + 12
= 25

4.​ engagement_ratio
Formula: user_turn_count / total_turn_count Example: 13 / 25 = 0.52
Meaning: 0.52 means user sent 52% of all messages Higher = more engaged
Lower = user barely responding, assistant carrying the conversation

5.​ avg_user_msg_length
Formula: mean of word count across ALL user messages in that conversation
Example: Turn 1 user: "I feel sad" → 3 words Turn 3 user: "Everything has
been overwhelming lately" → 5 words Turn 5 user: "ok" → 1 word avg = (3 + 5
+ 1) / 3 = 3.0 words

3

6.​ avg_assistant_msg_length
Same formula but for assistant messages only Formula: mean(word_count)
for all assistant turns in conversation

7.​ user_msg_length_std
Formula: standard deviation of word counts across user messages Example:
Word counts per turn: [3, 5, 1, 8, 2] std = how much these vary from the
average High std = user's message length is very inconsistent (erratic) Low
std = user writes similar length messages each time

8.​ last_user_turn
Formula: max(turn_number) where speaker == "user" Example: If
conversation has 25 turns and user's last message was at turn 22 →
last_user_turn = 22

9.​ last_speaker
Who sent the very last message in the conversation Formula: speaker of the
row with highest turn_number Values: "user" or "assistant"

10.​ ended_by_user
Formula: 1 if last_speaker == "user" else 0 Meaning: 1 = user sent the final
message (normal ending) 0 = assistant sent final message and user never
replied (possible dropout)

11.​ msg_length_slope
Formula: Linear regression slope of word_count over turn sequence
Example: Turn 1: 15 words Turn 2: 12 words Turn 3: 8 words Turn 4: 4 words
Turn 5: 2 words Slope = -3.2 (negative = shrinking = USER WITHDRAWING)
Turn 1: 3 words Turn 2: 6 words Turn 3: 10 words Turn 4: 15 words Slope =
+4.0 (positive = growing = USER OPENING UP) This is your most important
Gap 1 feature. Negative slope = possible depression/withdrawal signal

12.​ dropout_gap
Formula: total_turn_count - last_user_turn Example: Conversation has 25
turns total User's last message was at turn 20 dropout_gap = 25 - 20 = 5
Meaning: 5 assistant messages went unanswered at the end High gap = user
stopped responding = dropout signal

13.​ dropout_flag
4

Formula: 1 if dropout_gap > 2 else 0 Example: dropout_gap = 5 →
dropout_flag = 1 (user likely dropped out) dropout_gap = 1 → dropout_flag =
0 (normal ending)

What the Fusion Model Actually Does
Think of it like three doctors examining the same patient simultaneously:
Doctor 1 — Reads what the patient WROTE
Doctor 2 — Observes HOW the patient communicated
Doctor 3 — Looks at the patient's FACE

All three give their opinion → Chief Doctor makes final decision
That "Chief Doctor" is your fusion model.
